# The Story Behind The Journey

## Before Achievement, There Was Curiosity

Before Olympiads.

Before competitions.

Before awards.

Tin was simply a child who loved asking questions.

He was fascinated by nature, animals, dinosaurs, science documentaries, books, and the countless mysteries hidden in everyday life.

Unlike many children who ask questions and quickly move on, Tin often wanted to go deeper.

Why does this happen?

How does it work?

What causes it?

What would happen if we changed it?

These questions became the starting point of everything that followed.
